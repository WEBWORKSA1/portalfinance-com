Questions

Is PortalFinance free?

Yes. Every calculator, guide and market page is free and needs no account. The site is funded by advertising, sponsorships and reader support.

Do you store the numbers I type into calculators?

No. Calculations run in your browser. Inputs are kept only in the page address so you can bookmark or share a result.

Are you a bank, adviser or lender?

No. PortalFinance.com is an independent publisher of tools and education. If you request a free plan, we can introduce you to licensed professionals, but we never hold money or sell financial products ourselves.

How current is the market data?

Charts and quotes come from TradingView widgets, CoinGecko and European Central Bank reference rates. Some quotes may be delayed by the source.
