Money decisions deserve real numbers.

PortalFinance.com is an independent publisher of free financial calculators, market data and plain-English guides. We exist so anyone can check the math before they sign a loan, open an account or make a plan.

What we do

  • Calculators that show their formulas, run in your browser and never store what you type.
  • Live markets from established data providers, clearly attributed.
  • Guides that answer one question completely and link their sources.
  • A free financial game plan that turns answers into next steps and, only on request, an introduction to a licensed professional.

Editorial policy

Every guide is written and reviewed by the PortalFinance Editorial Team against primary sources: regulators, central banks, statistical agencies, official program pages and peer-reviewed research. We prefer original data to commentary, and we link to it.

  • Accuracy: figures are checked before publishing and reviewed at least once a year, or sooner when rules change. Each guide shows its last-updated date.
  • Independence: advertisers and partners cannot review, edit or approve content, and they never influence calculator math.
  • Clarity: we write for a smart reader who isn't a finance professional. No jargon without a definition.
  • Corrections: if we get something wrong, we fix it promptly and note significant corrections on the page. Report an error through our contact form.

How we make money

The site is free to use. It's funded by display advertising through Google AdSense, sponsorships that are always labelled, reader support, and referral fees from licensed professionals when a reader asks to be introduced through the free plan. Full details are in our advertiser disclosure.

What we are not

We're not a bank, lender, broker, investment adviser, insurer or crypto platform. We never hold your money, and our content is education, not personalised advice. See our disclaimers.
