Questions about the free plan

Is the financial plan really free?

Yes. There's no fee and no card required. We're funded by advertising, sponsorships and referral fees from partners you choose to talk to.

Will I get sales calls?

Only if you ask for a call or an introduction. Choose "Email me my written plan only" and we'll stick to email.

Is this personalised financial advice?

No. Your plan is general education based on your answers. For advice on your specific situation, speak with a licensed professional; we can introduce you to one.

Can I delete my information?

Yes. Use the contact form and choose "Privacy request". We'll confirm deletion within 30 days.
