Reading financial news well

Headlines are written to be clicked. Before acting on one, ask three questions: Does this change my goals or timeline? Is it already reflected in prices? Would I make the same decision if I hadn't seen it? For most long-term plans, the answer is no. The guides explain the concepts behind the headlines.

Headlines and summaries belong to their publishers and are linked for reference. PortalFinance.com does not reproduce full articles.
