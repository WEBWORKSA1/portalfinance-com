Support FAQ

Is my contribution tax-deductible?

No. PortalFinance.com is a for-profit publisher, not a registered charity, so contributions are not tax-deductible.

Do supporters get anything?

Patrons and sponsors can be listed on the supporter wall, and everyone gets a quarterly report on how the money was used. The tools stay free for everyone either way.

Can I cancel a monthly contribution?

Yes, at any time, by replying to any receipt or using the contact form.
