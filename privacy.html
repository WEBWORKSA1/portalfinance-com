Privacy & cookie policy

Effective November 12, 2026

This policy explains how PortalFinance.com ("PortalFinance", "we", "us") handles information when you use this website. We keep data collection to what's needed to run the site, answer you and fund it through advertising.

Information we collect

  • What you send us: when you submit a form (contact, newsletter, free financial plan, contest entry, job application, support pledge or partnership inquiry) we receive the details you enter, the page you sent it from and the time.
  • Calculator inputs: calculations run in your browser. Inputs are kept only in the page address so you can bookmark or share a result; we do not store them on our servers.
  • Device storage: we use your browser's local storage to remember your theme, your cookie choice and whether you've seen our checklist offer.
  • Automatically collected data: our hosting provider and the third-party services below may log technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited.

How we use it

  • To reply to you, send the plan, newsletter or checklist you asked for, and run contests and hiring.
  • If you ask for an introduction through the free plan, to share your answers with up to three licensed professionals who can help. We never sell your personal information to data brokers.
  • To keep the site secure, prevent spam and improve our tools.
  • To show advertising that funds the site (see below).

Service providers

Form submissions are delivered to us through FormSubmit, a form-to-email service. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Other providers are listed under third-party content. These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info or, in Europe, youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you choose "Essential only" in our cookie notice, we ask Google to serve non-personalised ads. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland may also see Google's consent message, which lets you manage choices in detail.

Third-party content

Some features load content from other services, which may set their own cookies or log your IP address: TradingView (market charts), CoinGecko (crypto prices), Frankfurter/European Central Bank (exchange rates), YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode (videos, loaded only when you press play) and Google Fonts (typefaces).

How long we keep data

We keep messages and leads for as long as needed to handle your request and for up to 24 months afterward, unless the law requires longer or you ask us to delete them sooner. Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, California privacy laws, Canada's PIPEDA or Quebec's Law 25), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. To make a request, use the contact form and choose "Privacy request". We respond within 30 days.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. Contests are open only to adults.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please never send account numbers, passwords or government ID numbers through our forms.

Changes

We'll update the effective date above when this policy changes and highlight material changes on the site.
