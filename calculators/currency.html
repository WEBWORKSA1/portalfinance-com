Why your bank's rate is different

The rates here are mid-market reference rates: the midpoint between what buyers and sellers pay in wholesale markets. Banks, card networks and airport kiosks add a markup to that rate, often 1–4%, and sometimes a flat fee too. To see the real cost of a transfer, compare the amount the recipient gets against the conversion shown here.

The ECB publishes these rates around 16:00 Central European Time on business days. For live intraday quotes, see the forex cross rates on our markets page.

Currency FAQ

Why isn't my currency listed?

The ECB publishes reference rates for about 30 major currencies. For others, such as AED or LKR, use your central bank's published rate or a live quote.

What's the cheapest way to send money abroad?

Compare the total received amount across providers, not the advertised fee. Specialist transfer services often beat bank wire transfers on the exchange rate markup.
